Robot Sandwich Instructions

Assumptions

The robot is standing in front of a kitchen counter.
The robot's left hand and right hand are empty.
A loaf of sliced bread is inside a bread bag on the counter.
A jar of peanut butter is on the counter with its lid attached.
A jar of jelly is on the counter with its lid attached.
A butter knife is on the counter.
A clean plate is on the counter.
The robot can recognize and grasp objects.
The sandwich will be made with two slices of bread, peanut butter, and jelly.
Steps

Place the plate on a flat area of the counter.
Locate the bread bag.
Grasp the twist tie or bread clip that seals the bread bag.
Remove the twist tie or bread clip from the bread bag.
Place the twist tie or bread clip on the counter.
Pull the opening of the bread bag apart until the opening is large enough for one hand to enter.
Insert one hand into the bread bag.
Grasp one slice of bread.
Remove the slice of bread from the bag.
Place the slice of bread flat in the center of the plate.
Insert one hand into the bread bag.
Grasp a second slice of bread.
Remove the second slice of bread from the bag.
Place the second slice of bread flat on the plate beside the first slice.
Close the bread bag opening.
Reattach the twist tie or bread clip to the bread bag.
Locate the peanut butter jar.
Grasp the peanut butter jar lid.
Rotate the lid counterclockwise until it separates from the jar.
Place the lid on the counter.
Pick up the butter knife.
Insert the knife blade into the peanut butter jar.
Move the knife blade through the peanut butter until peanut butter coats one side of the blade.
Remove the knife from the jar.
Move the knife above the first slice of bread.
Touch the peanut butter on the knife to the top surface of the bread.
Spread the peanut butter across the entire top surface of the bread.
Insert the knife blade into the peanut butter jar again.
Scrape any remaining peanut butter from the knife into the jar.
Remove the knife from the jar.
Place the peanut butter jar lid on the jar.
Rotate the lid clockwise until secure.
Locate the jelly jar.
Grasp the jelly jar lid.
Rotate the lid counterclockwise until it separates from the jar.
Place the lid on the counter.
Insert the knife blade into the jelly jar.
Move the knife blade through the jelly until jelly coats one side of the blade.
Remove the knife from the jar.
Move the knife above the second slice of bread.
Touch the jelly on the knife to the top surface of the bread.
Spread the jelly across the entire top surface of the bread.
Insert the knife blade into the jelly jar again.
Scrape any remaining jelly from the knife into the jar.
Remove the knife from the jar.
Place the jelly jar lid on the jar.
Rotate the lid clockwise until secure.
Put the knife on the counter.
Lift the slice of bread containing jelly.
Turn the jelly-covered surface toward the peanut-butter-covered surface.
Place the jelly-covered slice directly on top of the peanut-butter-covered slice.
Release the slice.
Done When

The sandwich consists of two slices of bread on the plate with peanut butter and jelly between them, and all jars have their lids attached.
